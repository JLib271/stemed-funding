    Where the listings come from

    Federal: calls from Grants.gov, where NSF, NIH, USDA, NOAA, NASA, the Department of Education, and other agencies post their calls. They are found by keyword and kept by title, so a call can be missed or, less often, listed when it is not about education. Checked every day.

    Foundations, societies, and state programs: programs kept by hand, each checked against the funder's page on the date shown. Programs between cycles stay listed so you know what is coming.

    Disciplines use the same categories as STEMEd+ Jobs, the STEMEd+ Digest, and the STEM+ Education Research Graduate Programs list, including History & Philosophy of Science, Human Dimensions, and Science & Health Communication, which cover those fields themselves as well as teaching in them. Programs open to all STEM+ fields appear under every discipline. For federal calls, disciplines come from the title and "What it funds" is matched automatically, so treat both as a guide.
